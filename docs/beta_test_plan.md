# Beta Test Plan

## Objective
To conduct a controlled, closed beta test to evaluate the real-world efficacy, engagement, and usability of LinguaLearn Engine v1.0.0 and its core language plugins, ensuring we collect actionable feedback before scaling to a public release.

## Target Audience
- **Size:** 20–50 users.
- **Demographics:** Absolute beginners (CEFR A0) with no prior knowledge of the target language. Mix of mobile and desktop users.
- **Languages to Test:** French, Spanish, German, Japanese.

## Testing Duration
- **Duration:** 14 Days (Two Weeks).
- **Start Date:** TBD.
- **End Date:** TBD.

## Daily Usage Expectations
- **Frequency:** Users are expected to complete at least one lesson per day.
- **Time Commitment:** Approximately 5–15 minutes of active learning per day.

## Test Scenarios
Beta testers will be asked to complete the following specific scenarios during the trial:
1. **Onboarding & First Lesson:** Start a new language course and complete the first lesson.
2. **Audio Interaction:** Complete a speaking exercise (if on a supported browser/device) and listen to vocabulary pronunciation.
3. **Cross-Device usage:** If possible, test the platform on both a desktop browser and a mobile device.
4. **Mistake Recovery:** Make intentional (or unintentional) mistakes and verify the Mistake Notebook logs them.

## Exit Criteria
The Beta test will be considered complete and successful when:
1. At least 70% of the active cohort completes the full 7-lesson A1 curriculum.
2. All submitted bug reports have been categorized and prioritized.
3. The core `success_metrics.md` data has been aggregated and analyzed.
4. A final "Beta Retrospective" meeting determines if the engine is stable enough for Phase 6B feature work (like Spaced Repetition).
