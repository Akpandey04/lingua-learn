# Feedback Framework

## Objective
Establish a structured approach for capturing, categorizing, and prioritizing feedback from beta testers to ensure actionable improvements to the LinguaLearn platform.

## Feedback Collection Channels
- **In-App Reporting:** A simple feedback button accessible during or immediately after a lesson.
- **End-of-Beta Survey:** A comprehensive questionnaire sent on Day 14.
- **Direct Interviews (Optional):** 15-minute syncs with highly engaged (or highly frustrated) users.

## Feedback Categories

To process feedback efficiently, all incoming reports must be tagged into one of the following categories:

### 1. Educational & Content Issues
- **Confusing Lesson:** The objective or grammar rule wasn't clear.
- **Hard Vocabulary:** Words that are too difficult or introduce cognitive overload.
- **Translation Issue:** Unnatural English translations, literal mismatches, or context errors.
- **Memory Tip Issue:** Mnemonic was unhelpful, confusing, or culturally irrelevant.

### 2. Technical & Audio Issues
- **Audio Issue (TTS):** The voice sounded robotic, incorrect, or failed to play entirely.
- **Speech Issue (Recognition):** The app failed to understand the user's correct pronunciation, or falsely accepted poor pronunciation.
- **Bug/Crash:** General platform instability, UI freezing, or lesson progression blockers.

### 3. User Experience (UX) & Motivation
- **UI Issue:** Poor mobile scaling, hard-to-read text, or confusing navigation.
- **Motivation Issue:** The user felt bored, discouraged, or lacked a reason to return the next day.
- **Pacing Issue:** The lesson felt too long, or moved too quickly through new concepts.

## Triage Process
1. **Log:** All feedback goes into a central tracking board.
2. **Categorize:** Assign one of the above tags.
3. **Prioritize:** 
   - *High:* Broken audio, crashes, blocking progression bugs.
   - *Medium:* Confusing translations, hard vocabulary.
   - *Low:* UI tweaks, new feature requests.
